About

DevSecOps Engineer & Scrum Master @ Swisscom · CAISP · MSc Cybersecurity · MSc AI (thesis deposited)

Profile

I turn security process into platforms. At Swisscom I work as a DevSecOps engineer and Scrum Master on two problems that do not scale with tickets: secrets at high assurance (HashiCorp Vault, including key-ceremony tooling) and security transparency (from team-level metrics to scheduled data products for leadership). Before that I was an Application Security engineer in insurance (SAST/DAST, vulnerability management, secure SDLC).

I have deposited an MSc thesis in Artificial Intelligence: an OpenAI-compatible AI Security Gateway (prompt threat detection, DLP, routing). I hold CAISP and hands-on DevSecOps certifications (API, containers, cloud native, threat modeling). CISSP is in preparation.
